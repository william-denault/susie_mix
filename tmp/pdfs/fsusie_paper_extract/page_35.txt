To derive our VA approximation of the posterior of model (12), we start by computing some key
quantities of some simpler models. These quantities will serve as building blocks for our estimation
procedure. Here, we consider that the matrices Y, D (response data) and X (covariates) are centered
in a column-wise fashion. Similarly the corresponding vectors Y, d (response data) and x (covariates)
are also assumed to be centered.
