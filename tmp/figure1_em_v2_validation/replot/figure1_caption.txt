Figure 1. SuSiE versus EM SuSiE-mix.
One parent cohort: 324 gene-tissue regions across 27 tissues, iteration 18. Minimum marginal additive P < 1e-08, mean reads >= 100, >=1 CS in either model. These are gene-tissue regions, not independent genes.
A. Overall CS-count agreement: 226 / 324 (69.8%). Four tissues with highest disagreement rate among tissues with >=10 cohort regions; ties use larger region count, then tissue name. The ranking cutoff affects only table selection, not other panels or the headline. Rows are SuSiE counts; columns are EM counts; cells are exact region counts. The 0/0 cell is outside the cohort.
B. Exact biological lead retention: 181 / 285 one-to-one matched CS pairs (63.5%), ignoring coding. Reserve identical variant IDs first, then minimize total physical distance for remaining CSs. ECDFs display changed pairs and extra CSs with a counterpart-model CS. Extra-CS distances are to the nearest other-model lead and are not one-to-one matches. 41 model-only extra CSs have no defined nearest lead and are omitted. 0 selected regions have unavailable matching and are audited.
B/C. ECDFs use log10(1 + distance in bp), retaining zero. Distinct alleles at the same coordinate are different leads with zero physical separation. Physical matching does not establish shared causal signals or LD.
C. Absolute lead-to-gene-TSS distance for precisely the two extra-CS groups in B, excluding 1 missing distances. Model-only regions remain excluded. The two ECDFs are descriptive, unmatched groups; TSS proximity is not a ground-truth accuracy measure.
D. Posterior coding support from the selected iteration's saved weighted_fit_mix fits in the same cohort. For each component l with a reported CS, q(l,c) = sum_j alpha(l,j) over ALL predictors j with coding c (additive, recessive, dominant), including predictors outside the reported CS. Average q equally across reported-CS components within each tissue. No PIP threshold, hard lead-coding assignment, CS truncation/renormalization, or refitting is used. 294 EM CSs from 286 / 324 cohort regions contribute. Regions with zero EM CSs have no component to average and remain in the region audit; tissues with zero EM CSs have undefined support, not zero support.
D pooled support, weighted by CS count: additive 81.8%; recessive 6.2%; dominant 12.0%. Additive probability remains in the denominator although only recessive and dominant probabilities are shown by tissue. Tissues are ordered by mean recessive+dominant posterior support. These are model posterior probabilities among reported-CS components, not the proportion of loci that truly have nonadditive effects.
The selected iteration is resolved once from completion markers, manifest and priors. Latest completed does not itself establish convergence across EM iterations. Coding priors are used only for provenance checks and are not panel D. Saved fit counts, component indices, memberships, lead IDs/codings and PIPs must match the EM summary.
