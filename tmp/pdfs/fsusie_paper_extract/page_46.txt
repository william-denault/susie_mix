Supplementary Figure 1. Additional assessment of SuSiE and fSuSiE CSs in simulated data sets, with
performance broken down by the true number of causal SNPs. Power is the proportion of the true causal
SNPs included in at least one CS; coverage is the proportion of CSs containing a true causal SNP . The dotted
horizontal line shows the target coverage (95%). In all simulation settings, the data sets were simulated withn =
100 samples, T = 128 CpGs, J = 1,500–4,000 SNPs, and 10% total variance explained. Each plot summarizes
the results from n = 300 simulations.
