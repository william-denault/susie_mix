 
Supplementary Figure 3. Comparison of power at a given false discovery proportion (FDP). Here , FDP 
is defined as the proportion of false genes among those predicted by a method at a given cutoff. For each 
method, we select genes by starting from the top of ranked gene lists generated by that method and stop when 
the given false discovery proportion was reached, then we count the number of causal genes that were found. 
The height of the bar is the mean over five independent simulations; the standard error is shown for each bar 
in vertical lines from the same five simulations. a and b. False discovery proportion 20%. c and d. False 
discovery proportion 40%. a and c, high gene PVE setting as in Figure 2. b and d, low gene PVE setting as in 
Figure 2.  Given the generally high false positive rates, alternative methods other than cTWAS often cannot 
reach FDP at 5% or 10%, no matter how stringent the thresholds are, so we compare the methods at FDP 
cutoff of 20% and 40%.
